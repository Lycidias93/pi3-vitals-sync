Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

Who operates the service

pi3-vitals-sync is a private, non-commercial synchronization service operated by Julien Asaël for the account owner's personal use. It is not offered as a service to the public. Contact: lycidias@gmail.com.

Information accessed

When the account owner authorizes the service with Google OAuth, the service can access only the Google account information and health-related information covered by the permissions shown in Google's consent screen and approved by the account owner. The exact data available depends on those permissions and Google's APIs. The service does not ask for or use Google account data outside that authorized purpose.

The service also uses OAuth authorization information, such as access credentials needed to keep an authorized synchronization working. The public information website does not provide sign-in, contact forms, analytics, or advertising.

How information is used

Google user data is used only to authorize the account owner's personal synchronization and to transfer the permitted health-related information for that purpose. It is not sold, used for advertising, or used to build profiles for unrelated purposes. The service does not use Google user data to train general-purpose AI or machine-learning models.

Google processes information as needed to provide its authorization and API services. A storage or processing provider may receive data only if the account owner has configured that provider as part of the personal synchronization workflow, and only for that workflow. No other person is given access by this service except where required by law.

Storage and retention

Authorization credentials and synchronized information may be held in the private environment and in any account-owner-controlled destination configured for synchronization. The service is intended for the account owner's use; it does not make health information publicly available. Retention of synchronized copies and backups depends on the destination and its settings. Operational records are limited to what is needed to run and troubleshoot the synchronization.

Security

Reasonable access controls and safeguards are used to protect the private synchronization environment. No internet-connected service can guarantee absolute security. The account owner should protect their Google account and any configured storage destinations.

Choices, revocation, and deletion

The account owner can stop future Google access by revoking the app's access in Google Account permissions. Revocation may stop synchronization but does not automatically remove copies already stored in a configured destination or its backups. To request deletion of copies held by the service operator, contact lycidias@gmail.com. Copies in third-party destinations must also be deleted there by the account owner, subject to those providers' retention and backup practices.

Where applicable, data-protection law may provide rights to access, correct, erase, restrict, or object to processing. Requests can be sent to the contact above.

Website hosting

This informational website is hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information under its own privacy and service terms. The website itself does not intentionally set analytics or advertising cookies.

Google API policy

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Changes

This policy may be updated if the service or its data practices change. The current version will be published on this page with its update date.
